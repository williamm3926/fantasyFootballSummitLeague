What if you had their schedule?

Every team's six weekly scores replayed against every other team's six opponents. 144 records, one grid. Week 7 is excluded because it is still in progress.

Power ranking

Sorted by average wins across all 12 schedules. Luck is actual wins minus that average: positive means the real schedule helped.

Schedule swap grid

Row = the team's scores. Column = whose schedule it plays. The outlined cell on the diagonal is the real record. Tap a team or a column header to focus it; tap a cell for the week-by-week games.

Tap any cell to see the six games behind that record.

How it is calculated

Team A on Team B's schedule: each week, A's actual score is compared to the actual score of whoever B played that week. When B's opponent was A itself, A plays B that week instead.

All-play: each team's score compared to all 11 other teams every week (66 games). It removes schedule luck completely and is the best single measure of who has actually been strongest.
